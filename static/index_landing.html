INTEGRATED COMPLIANCE MODULES

Flexible Analyst Workspace Suite

Tracy provides modular compliance tools that analysts can access in any order throughout their investigation.

Document Repository & OCR Parsing

Extract text, tabular data, and corporate structure directly from uploaded PDF packs, certificates of incorporation, and board resolutions with quote-level evidence.

Ownership Structure & UBO Mapping

Disentangle offshore holding companies, multi-layered trusts, and voting rights from the customer’s documents. The British Virgin Islands has no public shareholder register. Calculate effective ownership percentages and view entity geographic locations on a map.

Sanctions, PEP & Adverse Media Screening

Names are screened on the UN list Hong Kong implements, published by CEDB and the Security Bureau, plus OFAC, EU and UK. OpenSanctions is an extra sanctions file for this non-commercial demo, not a production license. PEP stays a labelled sample. Adverse media is a web search of the name only, and the officer confirms each lead.

Source of Funds & Wealth Analysis

Compare declared business activities against financial credits, banking records, and revenue disclosures to ensure legitimate wealth origination.

Risk Rating & Scoring Matrix

Compute dynamic risk scores considering country risk, entity complexity, PEP exposure, and financial velocity to suggest a transparent risk tier.

Automated Approval Memo & Sign-off

Generate audit-ready compliance approval memos summarizing findings, risk mitigations, officer notes, and sign-off recommendations.
